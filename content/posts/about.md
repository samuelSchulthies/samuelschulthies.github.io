## About Me

My name is Sam! I’m studying character FX in Brigham Young University’s computer science and animation program, and I expect to graduate in August 2027.

I’m passionate about fur and cloth simulation and creating elegant tools to solve technical problems. I love hiking, the mountains, and animals.

In summer 2025, I interned with Pixar’s systems team in IT support. I’m currently an animation systems administrator at BYU. Through my studies, I’ve worked in 3D across the animation pipeline, from concept to lighting and rendering.
